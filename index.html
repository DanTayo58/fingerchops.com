The Finger Chops Story

Built in Delta State, growing across Nigeria, and driven by a commitment to quality.

Founded in 2009, Finger Chops Ventures began as a food business specializing in indoor and outdoor catering services before expanding into full scale bakery operations to serve a wider customer market. Established in 2009 and fully incorporated with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), the company has grown into a trusted producer of premium quality bread.

Serving individual businesses and institutions across Delta and neighboring states in the Niger Delta region, Finger Chops continuously delivers fresh, nutritious, and hygienically packaged bread through continuous investment in mechanized equipment, skilled personnel, and high-quality ingredients.

The company's long-term vision is to transition into one of Africa's leading food manufacturing companies, renowned for producing quality and nutritious products from locally sourced agricultural commodities, while competing globally through operational excellence, innovations, and unwavering commitment to quality. Today, we are proud to serve Warri, Sapele, Asaba, Benin, Bayelsa, Port Harcourt, and Anambra.
