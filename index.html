The Finger Chops Story

Built in Delta State, growing across Nigeria, and driven by a committment to quality.

Founded in 2009, Finger Chops Ventures began as a food business specializing in indoor and outdoor catering services before expanding into full scale bakery operations to serve a wider customer market.
Established in 2009 and fully incorporated with corporate affairs commission (CAC), the company has grown into a trusted producer of premium quality bread serving individual businesses and institutions across Delta and neighboring states in the Niger Delta region. Through continuous investment in mechanized equipment, skilled personnel and high quality ingredients, Finger Chops bakery continuously delivers fresh, nutritious, and hygienically packaged bread.
The company's long term vision is to transit into one of Africa's leading food manufacuting companies, renowed for produing quality and nutritious products from locally sourced agricultural commodities, while competing globally through operational excellence, innovations, and unwavering committment to quality. Today, we're proud to serve Warri, Sapele, Asaba, Benin, Bayelsa, Port Harcourt, and Anambra

Loaves of Hope

Every week, we pack up crates of fresh, warm, nutritious Golden Butter bread and deliver them directly to local motherless baby homes, orphanages, and struggling family shelters in and around Warri. To us, feeding the community is a responsibility we carry with absolute pride.
